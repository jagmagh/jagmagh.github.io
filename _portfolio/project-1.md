description2: I assess the feasibility, scalability, availability, and technical sustainability of proposed solutions, conduct deep-dives into vendor proposals and perform FMEA, and develop risk mitigation strategies. Technology roadmaps must be grounded in factors like technology maturity, operational readiness, and cost sustainability — not just technical idealism.

description3: At SeaTech Consulting, I led the modernization of the organization's infrastructure from on-premises to Google Workspace and AWS Cloud, achieving 40% annual cost reduction.
