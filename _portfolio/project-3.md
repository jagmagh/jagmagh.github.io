description: Complex problems in software and architecture rarely have obvious solutions. My approach starts with identifying the real underlying issue — not just the symptoms — and developing pragmatic, phased strategies that minimize disruption while achieving meaningful change. This often means working closely with executive stakeholders to separate perceived pain points from root causes before any architecture work begins.

description2: At SeaTech Consulting, I led the incremental modernization of AeGIS (general insurance) and IRIS (reinsurance) core platforms — migrating from a 2-tier Gupta/Oracle client-server architecture to a modern API-oriented .NET platform using Domain-Driven Design. The modernization had to be executed without disrupting live insurance operations across multiple clients, requiring careful phasing and risk management over several years.

description3: At Accenture, I was the offshore engineering lead for BestBuy's Field Management System — a critical scheduling platform for Geek Squad technicians — leading a team of ~50 developers across geographies. Throughout my career I have consistently applied structured risk assessment to identify hidden technology and implementation risks before they become incidents, and built engineering processes that scale for teams of 10 to 75 people.
